Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a transformer repair typically take? expand_more
Minor fuse replacements are typically resolved within 2 hours. Major structural repairs may take 24-48 hours depending on parts availability.
How can I track my complaint status? expand_more
You can use our 'Track Progress' feature on the home screen using your Docket Number or registered mobile number.
Is there a priority service for hospitals? expand_more
Yes, essential services like hospitals and public security infrastructure are flagged in our system for top-priority emergency response.
