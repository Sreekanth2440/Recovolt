About RecoVolt

Modernizing Kerala's electrical infrastructure through digital innovation.

What is RecoVolt?

RecoVolt is a modern complaint management system designed for the Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB). It streamlines the process of registering, tracking, and resolving electricity-related complaints, providing a frictionless experience for both consumers and utility workers.

How It Works

1

Register & Complain

Consumers register securely and file detailed complaints, including location data and images.

2

Admin Reviews

The system routes the complaint to administrators who review and assign it to the nearest available technician.

3

Worker Resolves

Technicians receive digitized work orders, visit the location, resolve the issue, and update the status live.

4

Feedback

Upon resolution, consumers are notified and can rate the service, ensuring accountability.
